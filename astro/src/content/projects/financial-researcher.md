Financial Researcher is intentionally a smaller CrewAI experiment: a sequential researcher → analyst workflow with tool-enabled market research. It is useful as a baseline for comparing role handoffs and output quality against the larger hierarchical StockPicker system.

This portfolio presents the workflow through a deterministic replay. It does not provide investment advice or make a live market request.
