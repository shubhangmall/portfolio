Formulated a technical report providing a recommendation for a commercially available AC squirrel cage induction motor to be integrated with a specialized hoist tailored toward a customer’s unique application. The specialized hoist consisted of a speed reducer, a cable drum, and a single/multi-pulley cable assembly which had to operate within specified parameters.
